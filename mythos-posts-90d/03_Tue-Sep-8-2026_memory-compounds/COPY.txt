Notes pile up. Memory compounds.

Everyone has ten thousand notes and no memory. Storage was never the problem — you can save everything and still lose the thread, because a pile doesn't know what matters, what connects, what changed your mind.

Memory is different. Memory holds the decision AND the reason. The insight AND what it replaced. It gets more valuable every time you add to it, because everything lands connected to everything else.

That's the difference between a drive full of files and a substrate that knows you.

Storage remembers what you said. Memory remembers who you are.

When did a note you saved last actually come back to help you?
