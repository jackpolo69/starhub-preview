Your catalog will outlive every platform it was posted on.

MySpace died. Vine died. Platforms you're posting on today will die too — and every one of them takes a layer of context down with it: captions, threads, the connective tissue that made the work make sense.

Musicians learned this first. The catalog is the asset; the channel is just where it plays this decade. Creators are still learning it the hard way, rebuilding from screenshots every time a platform sunsets.

Build the canonical version of your work somewhere you own, and every platform becomes what it always should have been: distribution.

Platforms are venues. Your catalog is the music.

What work of yours already exists ONLY on a platform you don't control?
