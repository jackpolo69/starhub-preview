Your next pivot won't kill your brand. Losing the thread will.

Founders agonize over pivots like the audience is keeping score on consistency of PRODUCT. They're not. They're keeping score on consistency of PERSON.

Jobs went computers, movies, phones. Musicians change genres. The ones who survive reinvention all kept one thing intact: you always knew who was talking.

Pivot the product as many times as the market demands. Guard the through-line with your life.

People don't follow what you make. They follow who keeps making it.

What's the one thing about you that's survived every version of your work?
