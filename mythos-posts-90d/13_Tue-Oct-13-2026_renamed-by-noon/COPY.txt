We renamed a product in the morning. It was renamed everywhere by lunch.

Not just the logo. The app, the manifest, the docs, the demo sheet, the investor materials, the icons, the folder names on the desktop. One decision, propagated through every surface the brand touches, in hours.

Rebrands normally take quarters and still leave old names bleeding through PDFs for years. The difference isn't effort — it's architecture. When identity lives in one substrate, changing it is an edit, not an excavation.

Your brand should be as easy to change as your mind.

What's the oldest stale artifact still floating around your company?
