The metric nobody tracks: how much of you survives the algorithm.

Impressions, followers, engagement rate — all measurements of the megaphone. Not one dashboard on earth measures whether the person coming through it is still recognizably you.

Post enough algorithm-shaped content and the algorithm starts shaping you back. Slowly, then all at once, you're a channel instead of a person.

We started measuring the other thing: coherence across surfaces, drift from stated values, whether this month's voice would recognize last year's. It changes what you ship.

You become what you measure. Measure reach, become noise. Measure coherence, become unmistakable.

Would your last ten posts pass a blind taste test as YOURS?
