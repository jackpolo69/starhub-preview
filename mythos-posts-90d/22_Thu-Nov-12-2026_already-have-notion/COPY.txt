"I already have Notion and ChatGPT for that." You have a warehouse and a genius with amnesia.

The warehouse stores everything and understands nothing. Ten thousand notes, zero sense of what matters or how it connects. The genius understands everything and remembers nothing — brilliant at noon, blank slate by dinner.

The missing piece isn't a better warehouse or a smarter genius. It's the layer between them: structured memory of who you are that makes the storage meaningful and gives the intelligence continuity.

That layer is the actual product. Everything else is plumbing around it.

Storage without identity is a junk drawer. Intelligence without identity is a stranger.

What's the smartest tool you own that still knows nothing about you?
