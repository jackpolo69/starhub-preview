We don't delete old versions. We gray them out.

In our document libraries, a superseded agreement doesn't vanish. It stays on the shelf — grayed, struck through, unclickable — right next to the version that replaced it, with a note saying what changed and why.

Deleting history is the easy move and the wrong one. The record of how the thinking evolved IS the product: proof that the system corrects itself in the open instead of quietly overwriting.

Anyone can show you their current answer. The ones you can trust show you the path to it.

Supersession is the only honest form of erasure.

When did watching someone change their mind in public make you trust them MORE?
