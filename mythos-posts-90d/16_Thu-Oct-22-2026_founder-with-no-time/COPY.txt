She runs the business all day. The system holds the thread all night.

One of the founders we build with is in the field every single day — placements, calls, families in crisis. Zero hours left for "brand strategy." She is the exact person every identity guru's advice quietly excludes.

So the substrate does the remembering. Her positioning, her voice, her boundaries about what the company will not yet claim — held in the system, applied to every page and document that ships. She corrects it in minutes a week, not hours a day.

Coherence shouldn't be a luxury for people with time to curate themselves.

The busiest builders need the strongest thread — and have the least time to hold it.

Who do you know that's brilliant at the work and invisible because of it?
