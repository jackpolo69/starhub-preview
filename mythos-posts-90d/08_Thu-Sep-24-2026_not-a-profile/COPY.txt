An identity layer isn't a profile. It's memory with a spine.

When people hear "identity layer" they picture a fancier profile page. Name, bio, links. That's a business card, and it does what business cards do: nothing.

The real thing is alive. It holds your story and updates as it unfolds. It knows your values well enough to flag when something you're about to ship contradicts them. It carries your voice so tools can write WITH you instead of instead of you.

A profile describes you. A substrate represents you. The difference is everything.

Static is a snapshot. Sovereign is a system.

If your tools could know ONE thing about you reliably, what should it be?
