Empathy · The Pursuit of Joy — internal playbook

The influencer outreach machine.

Semi-automated on purpose: we automate the research, personalization, cadence, and tracking — the SEND comes from Natalie's own account, so it lands as a person, never as a bot. (Fully-botted IG DMs get accounts restricted and read as spam to exactly the people we want.)

How it runs, weekly

  1. Roster — researched target list (separate doc), 5 new contacts per day max, best-fit first
  2. Personalize — Nova drafts each message from the templates below, referencing the creator's actual recent content
  3. Send — Natalie (or Jack) sends from her IG / email in one 15-minute daily batch
  4. Track — every creator gets a personal link: …/journey?ref=theirhandle — clicks land in our analytics automatically; the tracking sheet records the rest
  5. Follow up — no reply after 5 days → one gentle nudge (template C). Never more than two touches.

The offer (proposed — Natalie rules on comp)

Partner offer, proposed
Per-seat partner thank-you: OPTION A — 10% per seat placed ($1,000 on a $10k / $2,000 on a $20k). OPTION B — flat $750/seat any tier. Both PLUS "supporting partner" film credit. Paid from the sale itself — zero budget out of pocket, fully aligned.
One question for Natalie's accountant before any comp is promised: trip contributions are tax-deductible donations to the film — paying a commission on a donation has tax/legal nuance (it may need to be structured as a marketing fee from the production company, or the referred seat treated as part-donation). The templates below promise "a partner thank-you we'll detail on our call" — nothing specific — until this is answered.
