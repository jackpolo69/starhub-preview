What Abeó owns · what StarHub owns · what is licensed

The working split, consistent with everything discussed — final language belongs in whatever agreement we land on. None of it changes with the deal shape: these commitments stand under a JV, a services agreement, or anything between.

Abeó owns — outright

  • The Abeó brand, name, mission, positioning and identity — Brittany created these; StarHub executed design and build against them
  • All Abeó-specific content, copy, imagery and documents
  • All Abeó business data: clients, providers, bookings, contacts, accounts
  • The abeo.health domain and its mail

StarHub owns — pre-existing

  • The underlying build engines and rendering system used to produce the site and documents (built before and beyond Abeó)
  • The deployment, preview and verification infrastructure
  • Reusable component patterns not specific to Abeó

Guaranteed either way

  • No captivity. Full export of site content, documents and data at any time, in standard formats — the relationship is never the hostage
  • Administrative access to Abeó's own accounts
  • Abeó's information is scoped to Abeó — it is not used to benefit any other company, and an inventory of where Abeó materials live can be produced on request
