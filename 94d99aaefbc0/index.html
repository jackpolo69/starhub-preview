StarHub Studios · concept brief · working title "Royalty Truth"

The money is made. The reporting hides it.

A reconciliation system for independent film distribution — built for the filmmakers the statements are lying to.

The problem, as filmmakers live it

A film goes out through a sales agent to 30+ platforms across the world. Quarters later, the statements arrive: "no sales." But the film charted, the reviews piled up, friends rented it in three countries. Somewhere between the platform and the producer, revenue became zero — and no single person can see the whole chain, so nobody can prove it.

What the system does

  • Ingests everything: platform statements (CSV, PDF, API), the distribution contracts, and independent audience signals — chart positions, rankings, review volumes, territory data
  • Reconciles per platform, per quarter: reported revenue vs. what the contract math and the signals say should exist
  • Flags the gaps with evidence: every discrepancy carries its trail — the contract clause, the signal, the math
  • Generates the follow-through: statement-level information requests citing audit rights, ready to send — turning "I feel cheated" into a documented claim
  • One dashboard, whole catalog: every film, every platform, every quarter — the honest ledger a producer has never had

Why us

This is the discipline our whole studio runs on: one living source of truth, nothing quietly deleted, every claim carrying its evidence. We already build reconciliation dashboards, document rooms, and audit trails for our partners — this points the same machine at distribution statements.

What we'd need to go from demo to real

  • 3–6 months of real platform statements for one film (any film, any platforms)
  • The distribution agreement(s) behind them
  • Two working sessions with someone who knows where the bodies are buried in the chain

From that: a working reconciliation of a real title — the proof that prices the product.
