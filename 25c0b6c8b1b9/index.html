◆ How it's paid for — the seat costs nothing to hold

The platform goes live and pays for itself — by a rule, not a rate: we won’t name a percentage of your tuition until we know what a seat actually earns you, because a share above your margin would mean every child we bring costs you money — and we won’t build that. The commitment up front: recovery is capped at one-third of your margin on enrollments the system creates, the $50,000 founding-partner principal is fixed — against a platform whose conservative market replacement value exceeds $250,000 — and the receipt is visible the day it clears. After that, the standard partnership share applies only while we’re actively growing you. No bill, no invoice, no check. If it never generates a dollar, you never owe one.

The honest math

How the money works — plainly.

1The retainer pays for itself

The platform returns 10–20 staff-hours every week — that’s $2,000–$4,000/month of payroll time already being spent on messages, follow-ups, and social. The technical-partner retainer is priced below that saving — so from month one, Wonderstorm keeps more cash than it does today. It isn’t a new expense; it’s a discount on an existing one.

2Marketing starts free, ads earn their way in

Phase 1 spends zero ad dollars — the storytelling engine, referral architecture, and instant-response funnel harvest the demand you already have. Paid ads only switch on at the gate where new revenue is flowing to fund them. The system never asks for fuel it hasn’t already generated.
