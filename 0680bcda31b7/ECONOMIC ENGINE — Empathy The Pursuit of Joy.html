section 02
Where the numbers come from

Attack these, not the totals.

These revenue lines are carried unchanged from your own one-sheet projections — deliberately. Winning this work on inflated numbers would be worth nothing to either of us six months in.

What drives the fan site

  • Launch rides the film's own moment: Aug '26 completion, Nov '26 DIFF premiere — the audience arrives before the storefront can go stale
  • Recurring membership, not one-time crowdfunding: backers fund the work continuously and receive the production feed
  • The 20 TB of footage IS the membership product — behind-the-scenes, Dharamshala, the Creative Music Lab

What drives merch &amp; subscription

  • Print-on-demand plus small-batch curated drops — 45% cost of goods, zero inventory risk, no capital tied up
  • The neuroscience-curated apparel line is a genuinely differentiated product, not tour merch
  • Sound-bath audio is the highest-margin line: near-zero marginal cost, and Maejor's involvement makes it a reason to subscribe

The honest risk: 2026 is only four months of trading and every line depends on the premiere landing. If DIFF slips, 2026 slips with it — the ramp shifts, it does not shrink. Nothing in the model assumes a streamer deal, a series order, or grant income.
