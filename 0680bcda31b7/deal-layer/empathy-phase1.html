section 01 · Scope & Custody
The mandate

Bounded producing custody — not ownership.

StarHub Studios accepts bounded producing custody of the existing EMPATHY archive for one purpose: discovering the strongest film the witnessed material can legitimately sustain.

◆ Sovereignty, stated plainly

Natalie retains authorship, underlying IP, and creative sovereignty throughout. Josh's existing ownership position is respected and unchanged by this engagement. StarHub will not manufacture missing events, conclusions, scientific claims, or narrative certainty. Custody is a responsibility we carry for a while — it is not, and never becomes, ownership.

Inputs: the ~25 TB archive, plus relevant documents, research, prior edits, decks, transcripts, music, rights information, and Natalie/participant testimony made available for the engagement. All materials remain the property of their current owners; access is for this engagement's purpose only.

section 02 · The Four Deliverables
What Phase I produces

Four artifacts, one decision.

Deliverable What it answers
1 · Source-of-Truth Story Graph What actually exists in the archive: the people, events, transformations, science, and music the footage witnesses — mapped, indexed, and traceable back to source material.
2 · Competing Paper Edits The strongest ways to tell it: multiple candidate traversals of the material — including the feature-length canonical cut and its alternatives — tested against each other on paper before a dollar of new production.
3 · Missing Story Beats Register What the strongest tellings still lack: named gaps — thresholds, ordeals, resolutions, connective tissue — each traced to the edit that needs it.
4 · Production Requirements Map What it takes to finish: the shooting, access, talent, music, and scientific collaboration each missing beat requires — the factual basis for a real production plan and budget.

Phase I is complete when Natalie can determine what movie the existing archive supports, which traversal is strongest, what remains missing, and what production is required to finish it.

section 03 · What Phase I Is Not
The non-promises, in writing

Protective of everybody.

Phase I does not include or promise: a final cut · a predetermined narrative · a final production budget · a final capitalization requirement · distribution · talent attachments · scientific conclusions · Phase II production.

◆ The million stays what it is

The current ~$1M figure remains Natalie's capitalization hypothesis. Phase I neither adopts nor revises it. The real number arrives the honest way: Archive → Discover the Movie → Production Mandate → Schedule + Budget → Capitalization. The budget determines the raise — never the reverse.

The broader relationship under discussion — film producing participation, the audience & commerce engine, music strategy, the platform — is preserved separately and negotiated on its own merits. Nothing unresolved is smuggled into this scope.

section 04 · Commercial Terms
The open questions — priced together, next conversation

Five variables, nothing hidden.

Variable To resolve
Phase I fee & payment structure A real professional fee for the work. Payment now, staged, or explicitly deferred — and any deferred amount accrues and becomes payable at the film's capitalization unless the contributor expressly elects to convert it into risk participation. Nothing works free by silence.
Duration & archive access Expected engagement window; how the archive is transferred, secured, and returned.
Producing roles during Phase I Named responsibilities — who holds story custody, who coordinates science and music threads, who owns the schedule.
Ownership of the work product Who owns the Story Graph, paper edits, registers and maps Phase I produces — and what license each party holds in them.
Carry into Phase II What, if anything, carries automatically into production — versus what requires a new, separately authorized production mandate.

This page becomes an agreement when the five variables above are resolved and both sides sign. Until then it is a shared map of the engagement being discussed — attorney review before execution.
