The Empathy Project · A Dalai Lama Blessing

Join a Once-in-a-Lifetime Journey with the Empathy Project

Ancient wisdom · proven by neuroscience · for your life

Want to see more empathy, compassion, and kindness in the world? Join a group of like-minded people — as part of the filming of Empathy: The Pursuit of Joy — A Hero's Journey Documentary.

The mountains of Dharamshala are not just a destination — they are a living story of resilience. You'll experience oracles, monks, nuns, scholars, art and music, and healing traditions thriving side-by-side with cafés, markets, and daily village life. This is where prayer flags whisper in the wind… butter lamps flicker in temple halls… and children laugh in schoolyards where compassion, empathy, and kindness are taught alongside mathematics, art, and science.

Be part of the filming of a movie on empathy, compassion, and self-regulation — and receive a life-changing blessing from His Holiness the Dalai Lama: a rare and meaningful encounter with one of the world's most respected voices for compassion and human understanding.
