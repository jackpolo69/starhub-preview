03 · How one person does this

Worth saying plainly, because the volume is the part that sounds implausible.

Everything above runs on mythOS — a substrate that holds each company's identity, decisions and history in one place — and Nova, the intelligence that works on top of it and produces the surfaces. It is not autonomous: I direct it and review every line before anything ships. The building is fast; the judgment is the work. That is why sixteen properties exist rather than two.

The practical consequence for you: a complete brand world — site, backend, documents, campaign — goes from nothing to deployed in days rather than quarters, and the client ships their own updates afterwards with one command. Mobile layout is verified across seven device widths before anything goes out; brand rules are enforced in code rather than in a PDF nobody reads.
